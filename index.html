01 Profile

A hands-on leader in an always-on industry.

Bhekumzi Mtshoko established Mtshoko Security Services in February 2013, after spotting a gap in the market for a security company that residential and corporate clients could genuinely partner with — not just contract.

He is a security specialist with more than 20 years of hands-on experience across commercial and residential security — owning, managing and working inside some of South Africa's leading security companies, which grounds his leadership in what actually works on the ground.

He still believes in a hands-on approach, and remains involved in the day-to-day running of the company he built.

“ Hands-on leadership isn't a slogan here — it's how the company is actually run, every single day.

“Twenty years building what works on the ground — and training the people who carry it forward.”
